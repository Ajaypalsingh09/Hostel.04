About Sardar Rajput Hostel

Sardar Rajput Hostel provides safe, affordable accommodation for students and working professionals. The hostel focuses on discipline, communal living and holistic student growth.

History

Founded in 2017, Sardar Rajput Hostel began as a small boarding house for students near the New Campus, JNVU. Over the years it expanded with new rooms, a mess and common facilities to support growing student needs.

The hostel has been run with a focus on respect, education and community service, hosting cultural events and supporting alumni activities.

Rules and regulations

  • Respect timings: lights-out at 11:30 PM; be quiet after 11 PM.
  • Keep rooms clean; no smoking or narcotics on the premises.
  • Visitors allowed in designated hours and common areas only.
  • Hostel property must not be damaged; report maintenance issues promptly.
  • Follow mess rules and maintain hygiene during meals.

Rules may be updated by management. Violation may lead to disciplinary action.
